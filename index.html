Background
A bit more about me
Yong Ling Zheng
Founder & AI Product Builder
Orlando, FL

I was born into a business family — nearly all my relatives run factories or businesses. From a young age, I knew I was meant to be an entrepreneur.

I studied Fashion Design and Business Management in college, and went on to build my own sportswear brand, factory, and e-commerce business. By 2012, I became one of the top sellers in dancewear on Alibaba.

In 2014, I immigrated to the U.S. I re-entered the workforce as an insurance and real estate agent, while also helping Chinese companies participate in trade shows and expand their business in the U.S.

During the pandemic, life took a difficult turn. I made the decision to leave my marriage and start over as a single mother — and I did.

I began learning programming, discovered the world of AI, and founded my own high-tech company. My story is just getting started.
