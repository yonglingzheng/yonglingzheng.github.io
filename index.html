A bit more about me:

I was born into a business family — nearly all my relatives run factories or businesses. From a young age, I knew I was meant to be an entrepreneur.

I studied Fashion Design and Business Management in college, and went on to build my own sportswear brand, factory, and e-commerce business. By 2012, I became one of the top sellers in dancewear on Alibaba.

In 2014, I immigrated to the U.S. and initially stepped away from business. However, during the pandemic, my ex-husband suffered major financial losses after shorting Tesla, which led to severe mental health issues and domestic violence. I made the decision to leave the marriage and start over as a single mother.

I re-entered the workforce as an insurance agent and real estate agent, while also helping Chinese companies participate in trade shows and expand their business in the U.S.

At the same time, I began learning programming and eventually founded my own high-tech company.
